# Acted reels — Instagram rescue

Four production scripts for actors. All four dramatise one pain: **you already
found the repo and judged it worth keeping, and then lost it in a saved folder
with no titles, no notes and no search.** None of them mention stars, credits or
highlights — that mechanic is deliberately absent.

| # | Title | Len | Cast | Locations | Feeling | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Standup | 35s | 2 | 1 | Humiliation, quiet | Medium |
| B | 2 AM | 30s | 1 | 2 (same flat) | Loneliness, bargaining | Easy |
| C | The Handoff | 25s | 2 | 1 | Social debt, comedy played straight | Easy |
| D | The Backfill | 20s | 1 | 1 | Earned delight | Easy |

**Post D last.** A, B and C are all pain; D is the only one that resolves, and it
pays off whichever of the others the viewer has already seen.

## Shoot order

B and D share a desk and a single actor — shoot them back to back in one evening.
A and C are the two-hander days. Every script's final shot is a screen, so all
four need the screens prepped **before** the shoot day, not after.

## Screens to prepare once, reuse four times

1. **Instagram Saved grid** — a real account, 40+ genuinely unlabelled tiles.
   Shoot the real thing. A mocked grid reads as fake within one frame.
2. **A source reel** — vertical, hand-held, demoing a parser, with the repo name
   **on screen but not in the caption** (this sets up `found on screen`).
3. **An Instagram DM thread** with a dated message from March.
4. **The repoherald list** — "Instagram finds", private, rows carrying a note and
   a `sent <date> · found in the caption / on screen / in the narration` line.
5. **The claim confirmation**, verbatim from `describeBackfill`:

   > @arjun.dev claimed. 9 repos you sent before claiming this handle are now in
   > your Instagram list, and 3 more are waiting to be reviewed.

   Keep the "waiting to be reviewed" clause. It is what the product really says,
   and the small honesty is what makes the nine believable.

## Direction philosophy

Three rules that apply to all four:

1. **Nobody in these scripts is a villain, and nobody is frustrated out loud.**
   Dev is kind, Sam is generous. All pressure is internal. The moment a scene
   partner becomes impatient, the audience watches a conflict instead of
   recognising themselves.
2. **Play the search, not the frustration.** Sighs, eye-rolls and "ugh" are how
   amateurs signal an emotion. Let the actor pursue the thing they want and let
   the feeling leak out at the edges.
3. **Hold the silences.** A (shot 10), C (shot 10) and D (shot 5) each have a beat
   with no line and no music. Those beats are the reel. Cutting them short to
   make the runtime is the one change that would break all four.

## Accuracy

The sweep reads posts **DM'd to the repoherald account** — it cannot read anyone's
own saved folder, and no script should imply it does. The turn in every reel is
"send it instead of saving it". Candidates are moderated before they become
listings, which is why D keeps the queue line on screen.
