# REEL A — "STANDUP"

**Length** 35s · **Cast** 2 · **Locations** 1 · **Difficulty** medium
**Emotional spine:** the quiet humiliation of knowing that you *knew*.

> **Logline.** In standup, Maya is asked for the library she is certain she saved.
> She cannot find it. Three weeks later, the same question, a different ending.

---

## CAST

**MAYA** — late 20s, backend developer. Quietly proud of being the person on the
team who knows things. She is not a show-off; being useful is how she feels safe.
**Objective in scene:** be the one with the answer.
**Obstacle:** she genuinely saved it, and it is genuinely gone.

**DEV** — 30s, tech lead. Warm, unhurried, *not* a villain. He is honestly asking.
His complete lack of judgement is exactly what makes it sting.
**Objective:** solve the problem and move the meeting along.

> **The most important note in this script:** Dev must never be impatient. If he
> is annoyed, the audience watches a bad manager. If he is kind, the audience
> watches themselves. All the pressure must come from inside Maya.

---

## SET, PROPS, WARDROBE

Small meeting room or a desk cluster — four people's worth of background life,
two of them actual bodies out of focus. Real laptops, open. One whiteboard with
something genuinely half-finished on it.

**Props:** Maya's phone (screen prepped, see SCREENS), her laptop, a coffee that
has gone cold. **Wardrobe:** ordinary. No branded tech hoodies, no lanyards.
Nothing that says "startup ad".

## ON-SCREEN TEXT

Only two cards, both lower third, both white on black:

- **11.0s** — "she saved it. she knows she saved it."
- **33.0s** — "repoherald · the stuff you sent, with the reason you sent it"

Nothing during shots 9–10. **That silence is the product pitch.** Let it be quiet.

---

## SOUND

Room tone only until 25.5. No music under the scroll — silence makes the scroll
unbearable, which is the point. Music enters *on the title card at 28.0*, warm and
low, and carries to the end. No sting, no whoosh, no riser.

---

## SCREENS TO PREP

1. **Instagram Saved grid** — at least 40 tiles, genuinely unlabelled, genuinely hers.
   Shoot the real thing on a real account. A mocked grid reads as fake instantly.
2. **The repoherald list** — "Instagram finds", private, with `tolerant-csv/parser`,
   the note *"The one that eats our broken export quoting."*, and the line
   *"sent 14 Mar · found in the caption"*.

---

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- Don't let Maya say "ugh" or sigh audibly. Sighing is how amateurs signal frustration.
- Don't cut away from shot 10 early. The whole reel is paying for that silence.
- Don't make the second scene a victory. It is a *non-event*. That is the promise:
  this stops being a thing that happens to you.
- No phone-screen zoom-punch effects. Shoot the screen properly in frame.
