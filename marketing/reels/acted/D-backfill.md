# REEL D — "THE BACKFILL"

**Length** 20s · **Cast** 1 · **Locations** 1 · **Difficulty** easy
**Emotional spine:** earned delight — the rare feeling of being given back something you'd written off.

> **Logline.** He claims his handle expecting an empty page. It isn't empty.
> **This is the only one of the four reels that ends happy, and it is the payoff
> reel for the other three. Post it last.**

---

## CAST

**ARJUN** — 20s–30s, mildly sceptical, has signed up for a lot of things.
**Objective:** get through a signup form.
**Obstacle:** none — and that's the point. The reel ambushes him with a good thing.

> **Casting note:** cast for the face. This reel is 60% one reaction shot. You need
> someone whose scepticism and whose delight both read instantly and small.

---

## SET, PROPS, WARDROBE

Desk, evening, one lamp. Laptop. **Nothing else matters.** Keep the frame tight and
uncluttered so the screen and the face are the only two things in the reel.

---

## SHOT LIST

| # | Time | Shot | Action | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–3.0 | MCU over shoulder | Account settings. A field: "Instagram handle". He types his. | Bored admin energy. He's done this a hundred times. Half-attention. |
| 2 | 3.0–5.0 | CU face | Clicks save. Already looking away, reaching for his drink. | **He is not expecting anything.** This disinterest is what sells shot 5. |
| 3 | 5.0–7.5 | Insert, screen | Confirmation: "@arjun.dev claimed. **9 repos you sent before claiming this handle are now in your Instagram list**, and 3 more are waiting to be reviewed." | Real copy from the product. Hold long enough to read the number. |
| 4 | 7.5–9.0 | CU face | He stops mid-reach. Looks back at the screen. | **The stop is the beat.** Not a double-take — a stop. Smaller is better. |
| 5 | 9.0–12.0 | CU face | He reads it again. Eyebrows. The beginning of a smile he doesn't perform. | **Do not smile at the camera. Do not react big.** Play it like someone alone in a room, because he is. The audience should feel like they're intruding. |

## ON-SCREEN TEXT

- **5.5s** — "he expected an empty page"
- **18.0s** — "you already sent these. we were already listening."

---

## SOUND

Room tone. A single warm chord enters at 7.5 — **exactly on his stop** — and swells
gently to the end. This is the only reel of the four that should feel scored.

---

## SCREENS TO PREP

1. Account page with the handle field.
2. **The real confirmation string**, which the product actually produces:
   `@handle claimed. 9 repos you sent before claiming this handle are now in your
   Instagram list, and 3 more are waiting to be reviewed.`
   Keep the "waiting to be reviewed" clause. It is true, and the small honesty is
   what makes the nine believable.
3. The list itself — nine rows, notes, dates spread across several months.

---

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- Don't cut to a wide shot. Stay close. The reel is a face and a screen.
- Don't speed-ramp the scroll in shot 6. Let it be slow and slightly boring — the
  length of the list *is* the payoff.
- Don't add a voiceover. This reel has no dialogue and should keep none.
