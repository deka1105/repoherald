# REEL C — "THE HANDOFF"

**Length** 25s · **Cast** 2 · **Locations** 1 · **Difficulty** easy
**Emotional spine:** the small social debt of a link you never opened.

> **Logline.** A friend asks whether you ever used the thing he sent you.
> You didn't. Not because it was bad — because you never found it again.

---

## CAST

**SAM** — 30s, generous, the friend who sends people things. He is not fishing
for thanks; he actually wants to know if it helped.
**Objective:** find out if the thing he sent was useful.

**RHEA** — 30s, the one who received it. Good at her job. Hates letting people down.
**Objective:** not admit she never opened it.
**Obstacle:** she is a bad liar and he is not accusing her of anything.

> This is a comedy reel played completely straight. Nobody is allowed to be funny.

---

## SET, PROPS, WARDROBE

A kitchen, a café table, or a sofa. Two people who know each other well — close
proximity, relaxed posture. Daytime. Mugs. **Props:** both phones, face-down on
the table at the start.

---

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- **Sam must never be smug.** The second he enjoys her discomfort, the audience
  sides against him and the reel breaks.
- Rhea should not over-apologise. One "send it again?" is the whole admission.
- Don't add a laugh track equivalent — no comedic sting, no zoom.
