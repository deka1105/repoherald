# REEL B — "2 AM"

**Length** 30s · **Cast** 1 · **Locations** 2 (same flat) · **Difficulty** easy
**Emotional spine:** the gap between the person who saves and the person who needs.

> **Logline.** At 2am she finds something genuinely good and saves it, happy.
> Six weeks later she needs it, and the version of her who saved it has left no note.

---

## CAST

**NOOR** — 20s–30s, developer. We only ever see her alone, which is the point:
this is the most private kind of frustration, the kind with nobody to blame.
**Objective (first half):** keep this. **Objective (second half):** find this.
**Obstacle:** she is two different people with no way to talk to each other.

---

## SET, PROPS, WARDROBE

**Scene 1 — bed, 2am.** Lights off. The only light source is the phone, under her
chin, hard and blue. Duvet. The specific posture of someone who said they'd sleep
an hour ago.

**Scene 2 — desk, daylight.** Same flat, wide open curtains, deliberately the
visual opposite. Laptop open, work on it.

**Props:** phone with a real reel playing, laptop. **Wardrobe:** sleep clothes,
then normal clothes. Same person, obviously different day.

## SOUND

Scene 1: near-silence, a clock, the faint tinny audio of the reel. Scene 2: room
tone, keyboard. **No music at all until 26.5.** Then one warm chord under the last
shot. The silence through the scroll is doing the work — do not score it.

## SCREENS TO PREP

1. A real-feeling vertical reel demoing a parser, with the repo name on screen but
   **not in the caption** — this sets up `found on screen` in the final shot.
2. Instagram Saved grid, 40+ unlabelled tiles.
3. Instagram search attempt returning nothing useful.
4. The repoherald list with the note and `found on screen`.

---

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- Don't show her face in shot 11. We're done with her — the screen is the ending.
- Don't make scene 2 a montage. One continuous failure is stronger than three.
- No alarm-clock sound effect. It's a cliché and it dates the reel.
